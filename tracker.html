Weekly rhythm

Weekdays (about 2 hours): 60 minutes of DSA against a timer, then 60 minutes on the week’s concept task. Saturday (4 hours): one timed design or machine-coding mock, then write down what broke. Sunday (3 hours): redo missed problems, update your notes and say one STAR story out loud.

Company tags mark where a topic is usually emphasised in loops. Treat them as a rough guide, not a rule. The problem bank and design prompts at the bottom of each category are for extra reps.

Progress is saved in this browser only. Brute-force first, then optimise: write the naïve solution, state its complexity, then improve it, for every DSA and design task.
